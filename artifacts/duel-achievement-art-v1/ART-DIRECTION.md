# Duel achievement art direction

Square, cinematic realistic hockey stills for small achievement cards. Keep the subject unmistakable at 160px: one decisive pose, puck, stick or goalie action, a deep navy arena and cold blue ice. Use restrained amber, red or warm-white highlights only to identify the moment. Photorealistic sports-poster finish, energetic ice spray and shallow crowd detail are welcome. Never use lettering, numerals, team logos, scoreboards, UI, badges, watermarks, or decorative frames.

The compositions deliberately differentiate each format and achievement: speed uses directional blur, streaks use sequential action, sniper work uses the goal mouth, marathon work uses pressure around the puck and rhythm uses repeated motion.
