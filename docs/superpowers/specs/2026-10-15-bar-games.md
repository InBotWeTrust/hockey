# Bar games — approved specification

Source: user-approved in-chat plan, 2026-10-15.

- Maria/darts, Veronika/eight-ball, Arsenich/Russian checkers; Daria alone retains Durak. Other characters have no game actions.
- Free character matches and online two-player invitation/search matches. No ratings, rewards, blackjack, spin, or online rematch.
- Darts: 301, three darts, single-out, bust ignores only that dart. Swipe direction/speed, deterministic input mapping.
- Eight-ball: portrait top-down, aim plus release power slider, short first-contact guide. Break leaves groups open. First legally pocketed ball after break assigns groups. Scratch/wrong or missing contact/no rail-or-pocket is foul, opponent has ball in hand. Early eight or eight with scratch loses. Eight on break is respotted. No called pockets or four-rail break requirement.
- Russian checkers: mandatory capture, backward man captures, flying kings, immediate crowning during capture, any complete capture path. Threefold repetition or 60 king-only noncapture plies draws.
- 40 seconds per human turn in both modes, excluding committed animation. First timeout: remaining darts miss / pool foul / first stable legal checkers move. Second consecutive timeout loses. Manual dart or full move resets streak. No pause for help, hidden tab or disconnection.
- Ready flow online as cards; character starts immediately. Random initial starter, alternate on character replay. Explicit surrender/exit confirmation. Results: character replay/back, online acknowledge/back.
- Help icon/modal with close in header. Character portrait and nonblocking authored reactions.
- Deterministic shared engines, server authority and durable revisioned/idempotent commands for new games, game-specific queues and cross-card admission locks. Preserve old cards APIs/history.
- Art: 3 backgrounds, 3 character invite scenes, 7 character results, 7 online results. Identity references from existing assets; no text baked into art. Daria reuses existing card art. Authored persona-consistent copy.
- Isolated feature/bar-games from fresh origin/dev. Dev only; no production mutation. Tests plus actual-browser acceptance; report missing physical-device or authenticated checks separately.
